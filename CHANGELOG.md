# Changelog

What's new on files.gg, newest first.

## 2026-11-25: The new files.gg

files.gg is rebuilt from the ground up: a new viewer, a dashboard, accounts, and much more.

- **Upload files up to 1 GB** with an account, or up to 25 MB without one. Drop, paste or pick files; big uploads resume if your connection drops.
- **Choose who can open each file:** anyone with the link, people with a password (we can generate one for you), or only you.
- **Expiry and view limits:** let a file expire after a set time, or after a number of people have opened it.
- **Albums** with grid, list and tracklist layouts, captions, and Download all.
- **Previews** for images, video, audio, PDFs, text and code, and Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents.
- **Convert files** between formats: images, GIFs, video, audio, PDFs and documents, with one-click presets such as "Make it play everywhere" and "Shrink for Discord".
- **Accounts** with email or Discord, and a dashboard for your files, albums and storage. Download your data or delete your account from Settings.
- **Upload keys** for ShareX and other tools, with a ready-made ShareX config.
- **Premium** with more storage, bigger uploads and **custom domains**, so your links can use your own domain.
- **Safety:** every upload is scanned before it's shared, adult content is labelled, and anyone can report a file, album or user. Follow your reports in My reports, and see and appeal actions on your files in Notices.

files.gg logo concepts

Six directions in two rounds (lowercase f first, then uppercase F) that keep what people already know about files.gg (the bright green, the rounded F built from strokes, and .gg) while working better on a dark dashboard and as a 16px favicon. The wordmark is set in Outfit for now; the final version will be outlined so it doesn't depend on a font.

Current: gradient paper-fold F and a serif "gg". The fold gradients get muddy below ~32px, and the serif doesn't match a modern UI font.

Round 3: Tile f. vs. Folded tile f.

A plain "lowercase f in a rounded square" has the same outline as Facebook's older app icon, and outlines are recognised before colour at small sizes. Folding the top-right corner gives the mark an outline of its own and makes it read as a file.

# Link warnings

files.gg checks the links in text files, PDFs and album descriptions against [Google Web Risk](https://cloud.google.com/web-risk), Google's list of sites known to be unsafe. When a link is on that list, files.gg shows a warning next to it and won't open it for you. You can still copy it if you're sure.

A warning means Google's list flags the site. It isn't proof: lists like this sometimes flag a safe site and sometimes miss an unsafe one, so a link without a warning isn't guaranteed to be safe either.

Each warning says which kind of problem Google suspects. They're explained below.

## Malware

**What it means.** The site may host malware: software that damages your device, steals your files or passwords, or lets someone else control your computer. Visiting such a site can be enough to get infected if your browser or system is out of date.

**What to do.**

- Don't visit the site, and don't download or open anything from it.
- If you already downloaded something, delete it without opening it.
- If you opened it, run a scan with your antivirus, update your browser and system, and change the passwords you use on that device.

## Social engineering

**What it means.** The site may be deceptive, often called phishing: it pretends to be something you trust (a bank, a game, an email or social media sign-in, a delivery service, files.gg itself) to make you type in a password or card number, or to install something.

**What to do.**

- Don't sign in, pay or download anything there. To reach a service you use, type its address yourself or use its app.
- If you already entered a password, change it on the real site now, and anywhere else you use the same one. Turn on two-factor authentication.
- If you entered card or bank details, contact your bank.

## Unwanted software

**What it means.** The site may offer software that does things you didn't agree to: it changes your browser's home page or search engine, shows ads, collects information about you, or is hard to remove. It's often bundled with something you did want.

**What to do.**

- Don't install anything from the site.
- If you already did, uninstall it, reset your browser's settings, and remove browser extensions you don't recognise.

## Potentially harmful applications

**What it means.** The site may offer phone apps, usually Android apps, that can harm your phone or your data: they steal information, sign you up for paid services, or open the phone to others.

**What to do.**

- Don't install apps from the link. Get apps from your phone's official store.
- If you already installed one, uninstall it, and check your phone bill and accounts for anything you didn't do.

## What files.gg does

- A flagged link is never a live link on files.gg: in text, PDFs and albums it can only be copied.
- A warning shows only while Google's answer about the link is current (each answer says how long it holds), and files.gg checks flagged links again to renew it. When Google's list no longer flags the site, the warning goes away.
- Our staff are told about every flagged file and album, and remove the ones that are there to harm people.

## If you think a warning is wrong

files.gg can't remove a warning while Google's list still flags the site. If the site is yours, check it for anything that was added without your knowledge, then ask Google to review it from the Security issues report in [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console). If a file on files.gg is harmful and has no warning, [report it](/report).
